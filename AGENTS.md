## Kubernetes change approval

Before making any Kubernetes mutation for this project, present the exact
cluster, namespace, resource, operation, and proposed manifest or diff. Wait
for a separate explicit approval of that specific plan. This also applies to
creating or deleting demo resources, using kubectl or the Kubernetes SDK
directly, and making changes through the troubleshooting agent. A request to
deploy, fix, or clean up authorizes investigation and preparation of a plan.
It does not authorize execution. In the chat application, approval must come
from the plan-specific approval control. Read-only discovery and previews may
run before approval.
